====================================================
WIRING NOTES - ROBOTIC ARM + CONVEYOR SUBSYSTEM
NEXUS-TN Project
====================================================

--------------------------------------------------
1. POWER SUPPLY ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------

This subsystem uses 4 SEPARATE LiPo battery cells/packs instead of a
single battery + voltage regulator setup.

Reason for this choice:
- No voltage regulator needed -> less heat loss, less wasted energy
- Saves physical space inside the arm/conveyor enclosure
- Each voltage rail gets its own clean, dedicated power source instead
  of being stepped down from a single higher-voltage battery

--------------------------------------------------
2. VOLTAGE RAILS
--------------------------------------------------

RAIL 1 - 7V (LiPo)
  -> Powers: all servo motors on the robotic arm
  -> Used for: arm joints / gripper actuation

RAIL 2 - 11.1V (3S LiPo)
  -> Powers: DRV8825 stepper motor driver
  -> The DRV8825 drives TWO motors:
       a) Conveyor belt motor
       b) Stepper motor moving the robotic arm along the
          linear XX' axis (leadscrew / nut mechanism)

RAIL 3 - 3.7V (1S LiPo)
  -> Powers: ESP32 control board (logic / microcontroller)

--------------------------------------------------
3. CONTROL & VISION ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------

Camera: Intel RealSense (depth camera)

Signal path:
  Intel RealSense Camera --> Intermediate PC --> ESP32 (control board)

Notes:
- A PC is used here INSTEAD OF a Raspberry Pi.
--------------------------------------------------
4. GENERAL WIRING NOTES
--------------------------------------------------

- All rails use SEPARATE positive lines (7V / 11.1V / 3.7V), but should
  share a COMMON GROUND (GND) between the ESP32, the DRV8825 driver,
  and the PC interface, to keep signal references consistent.
- Always confirm LiPo cell balance/voltage before powering the boards.
- Double-check DRV8825 current limit (Vref) setting before connecting
  the conveyor/XX' axis motors, to avoid overheating the driver.
